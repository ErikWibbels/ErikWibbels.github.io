Week 13 Assignment

Turn in your assignment by Tuesday, 11/24 at 5p.

Your assignment this week is to uncover some basic information on the amount and nature of foreign aid that your country receives.

1. Official Development Assistance Over Time

Provide a graph of the amount, in dollar and per capita terms, of Official Development Assistance (ODA) that your place has received over the most recent 10 years. Use the OECD’s statistics to find the data.

Discuss any trends you see in the time series. If there are sharp changes, discuss why they exist; if there are not, do you find the current level of support appropriate in light of your place’s challenges? Do you consider this a lot of money in light of what you know about your place?

2. Aid by Sector

Provide a graph of the distribution of spending across aid categories (or sectors) for the most recent year available. Only do the major groupings, not the sub-groupings.

Does the distribution across different aid categories reflect your understanding of your country’s challenges? Why or why not?

3. US Foreign Assistance Cuts

Assess the extent of US foreign assistance cuts to your country at ForeignAssistance.gov. What sectors were hit hardest?

Spend some time looking at reporting from media sources in your country to assess coverage of the impact of the cuts. What is the substance and tone of that coverage?

4. Chinese Development Finance

Go to AidData’s website on Chinese development project locations. Take a screenshot of Chinese project locations in your country.

Is China active in your country? If so, what and where is it spending its money on? Do its priorities seem to be different from what you found in the OECD and US data? If so, why? If not, why not?

Finally, do some desk research to find out if China has shown any indication of filling the gap left by US foreign aid cuts in your country.

Week 11 Assignment

Turn in your assignment by Tuesday, 11/3 at 5p.

This week’s assignment has two goals: to think carefully about (1) the practical challenges associated with the implementation of your intervention, and (2) any potential ethical concerns that your intervention might involve.

1. Revisions

Revise anything from previous assignments that you’d like comments on.

2. Implementation Challenges

Think carefully about how to ensure the successful implementation of your project, including potential sources of non-compliance among implementers and target populations. A good place to start is to think about the actors involved in the roll-out of the intervention and identify potential points of failure. Develop a monitoring strategy to increase your chances of detecting implementation problems. Discuss all challenges and potential solutions.

For a helpful practical guide, see the section on “Implementation Challenges” in this J-PAL guide.

3. Ethical Implications of the Intervention and Your Impact Evaluation

Discuss the ethical implications of your intervention. Think carefully about the nature of the intervention and how it impacts the treated population and the control. Pay attention to local power relations, your power as an evaluator, and your role as an outsider.

Review the lecture notes and see this helpful J-PAL lecture.

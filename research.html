Research

My research examines development, redistribution, and political geography — how markets, institutions, and geography shape governance and well-being, and how field experiments and machine-learning methods can be used to design and evaluate policy responses.

Papers still in progress — under review, revise-and-resubmit, or in preparation — are listed separately on the Working Papers page.

Policy Work

Alongside my academic research, I work directly with governments, international organizations, and implementing agencies to design and evaluate development and governance programs — and to translate research findings into policy.

I co-direct PDRI-DevLab, Penn's Development Research Initiative, an applied research hub that brings together social scientists and development practitioners to design rigorous programming, collect monitoring and evaluation data, and carry out impact evaluations of development projects. I also co-founded the Forced Displacement Working Group (with Claire Adida, Thomas Ginn, and Guy Grossman), a joint academic-policy forum with members from IOM, the World Bank, UNHCR, and the IFRC that meets twice a year to connect frontier research with policy needs, and I serve on the Migration Unit Advisory Group of the Inter-American Development Bank. Over the years this work has been supported by USAID, the World Bank, the U.S. Institute of Peace, RTI International, and the European Commission, among others.

Priority Projects

  1. Big Data for Development — Machine Learning for Peace (MLP) is a real-time monitoring platform, built with the INSPIRES consortium, that uses machine learning and NLP on over 120 million news articles to track civic-space restrictions and resurgent authoritarian influence across 60+ countries, feeding early-warning dashboards for policymakers and civil society.
  2. Migration, Displacement, and Deportation — evidence on the economic reintegration, security, and remigration decisions of Central Americans deported from the United States, aimed at informing USAID, State Department, and multilateral responses to migration policy; and, with the World Bank's Social Cohesion and Resilience Group and UNHCR, synthesis and original research on how refugee and IDP populations affect host-community attitudes, services, and politics.
  3. Governance & Accountability — large-scale impact evaluations of local governance, policing, and anti-corruption programming conducted with USAID and the World Bank in Ghana, Cambodia, Serbia, Indonesia, and the West Bank.
  4. Climate, Land, and Resource Governance — work with PDRI-DevLab and Penn's Kleinman Center on land tenure, deforestation-free supply chains, and the links between climate change, adaptation, and migration, including MLEED (Machine Learning for Environmental Event Detection), which tracks individual, community, private-sector, and governmental responses to climate change.
